# Design System

## Brand & Style
The design system embraces a high-contrast, professional, and reliable corporate-modern aesthetic with a dark color mode foundation. It conveys stability, precision, and authority through structured layouts, sophisticated typography, and purposeful color usage.

## Colors
- **Color Mode**: Dark
- **Primary Color**: `#3366cc` (Confident blue for primary actions)
- **Secondary Color**: `#54595d` (Neutral gray for supportive surfaces)
- **Tertiary Color**: `#fee77e` (Vibrant highlight)
- **Neutral Color**: `#202122` (Deep background foundation)

## Typography
- **Headlines**: Noto Serif
- **Body**: Inter
- **Labels**: Public Sans

## Layout & Spacing
Built on a structured grid with consistent spacing tokens (`space-xs` to `space-xl`), ensuring balanced alignment across viewports.

## Elevation & Depth
Utilizes tonal layering and subtle borders rather than heavy drop shadows to establish hierarchy in dark mode.

## Shapes
Soft roundedness (`roundedness: 1`), featuring gentle 0.25rem to 0.75rem corner radii.

## Components
Includes standardized buttons, cards, inputs, and chips styled consistently with the typography and color tokens.
