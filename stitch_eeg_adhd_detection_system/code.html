III. Neurologist Interpretive Synthesis

Qualitative Electrophysiology: Resting awake 19-channel electroencephalogram recorded during eyes-closed vigilance control demonstrates a statistically robust, bilateral frontocentral excess of slow-wave theta rhythmicity (4.0 - 7.5 Hz), most pronounced at the vertex electrode (Cz) and midline frontal electrode (Fz). Quantitative Biomarker Correlation: The observed Theta/Beta Ratio (TBR = 4.85) is markedly elevated beyond age-matched pediatric normatives (+3.28 standard deviations; p < 0.001). Concomitant Frontal Alpha Asymmetry (FAA = -0.38 dB) indicates hypoactivation of the left dorsolateral prefrontal cortex. This neuro-oscillatory electrotype correlates with attentional gating inefficiency and fronto-striatal dopaminergic dysregulation, corroborating a DSM-5 diagnosis of Attention-Deficit / Hyperactivity Disorder (Combined Presentation).

Paroxysmal & Epileptiform Screen: Thorough examination reveals no epileptiform spikes, sharp waves, spike-wave complexes, or focal slowing. Background continuity is maintained without evidence of metabolic encephalopathy or generalized paroxysmal discharges.
